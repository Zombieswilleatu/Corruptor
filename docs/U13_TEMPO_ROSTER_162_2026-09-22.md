# Current tempo rules: 162-game roster screen

All 162 games completed under the current Python experimental rules, with two recycled workers. Two fresh seeds covered every ordered Lord matchup: 144 non-mirror games and 18 mirrors. Each non-mirror Lord pairing used the same seed in both seat orders. Every Lord appeared in 32 non-mirror games, equally divided between seats.

The results support decisive normal endings, but the target of **most games in rounds 15–20 is not yet met**.

| Finishing round | Games | Share |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Before 15 | 88 | 54.3% |
| 15–20 | 64 | 39.5% |
| After 20 | 10 | 6.2% |

Mean duration: **14.56 rounds**. Median: **14**. Range: **8–24**.

There were **95 Dominion wins (58.6%)** and **67 Ritual wins (41.4%)**. No game reached the forced round-25 ending. Final Collapse is disabled by this profile, so its absence alone is not evidence of success; the useful observation is that every game reached a normal victory before the cutoff. Of the 88 early games, 22 ended without a positive Veil attack bonus ever being applied.

## Seat results and source audit

| Group | Seat 0 wins | Seat 1 wins |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Non-mirrors | 70 / 144 (48.6%) | 74 / 144 (51.4%) |
| Mirrors | 9 / 18 | 9 / 18 |

Across 72 paired non-mirror seed comparisons, the same Lord won both seats in 40 pairs; seat 0 won both games in 15 pairs and seat 1 won both in 17. This sample does **not show a seat-0 win-rate advantage**, but cannot exclude a small underlying effect.

The source does contain explicit seat priorities:

- `u13_pysim/full_match.py`: the inherited next-round handler resets `player_order` to `[0, 1]`.
- `u13_pysim/effects.py`: effects within the same hook are sorted by that player order.
- `U13PendingEffects.gd`: the native equivalent explicitly calls this Reflex order.
- `U13GameConductor.gd`: starts and advances rounds with `[0, 1]` too.
- `u13_pysim/lifecycle.py`: simultaneous living Ritual qualifiers favor seat 0; equal souls at the round-25 cutoff also favor seat 0.
- Slaver first-visitor order is separately keyed and seeded; it is not the same mechanism.

No game in this sample had both players at the Ritual soul threshold at a Ritual ending, and none reached RoundLimit. Thus neither terminal tie preference determined a sampled winner. Earlier effects of fixed Reflex order remain unisolated. The audit required zero additional replays because there were no terminal tie candidates.

## Rules and validation

The profile retains paid Ward plus optional Hunt/Siege, disjoint shared-card payments, no Sigils, no Ward recipe summons, causal-save Ward souls, and the existing recruit ratios. Veil 13/17/21 adds flat +1/+2/+3 committed attack strength. Extra decisive souls start at round 20. Normal victories precede the hard round-25 cutoff.

This is **Python-only experimental gameplay**. Godot/playable integration is not claimed by this screen. The native source was inspected for the seat-order question; no native execution was available here.

57 focused tests passed. All 162 completed records passed frozen-source, semantic, trace, and operation-hash validation. There were 18 rejected admission previews; legal alternatives were selected, and no submitted game failed.

Two interrupted attempts exposed passive diagnostic accounting errors: coordinated Gremory powers and assembled monster recipes could be selected outside the standalone shortlist while the recorder still counted zero retained choices. Accounting now includes actual full-plan variants. Both triggering observations have regression fixtures. Neither fix changes game rules, plan scores, or selection. All 3 completed games from attempt 01 and all 31 from attempt 02 reproduce their operation and final-state hashes in the final run; the 18- and 20-decision failing prefixes also preserve every observation and chosen plan. Interrupted attempts are excluded from the 162-game sample.
