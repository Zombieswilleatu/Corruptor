**Unit balance audit — 18 September 2026**

Completed **13,056 seeded battle trials**, comprising 5,376 duels, 5,472 squad fights, 1,824 focused hunting trials and 384 mixed-monster fights. These simulated 57,988 Marching phases, or 11,597,600 movement/combat ticks.

**Recommendation:** fix Tumler's pursuit, then use targeted stat buffs. The results do not support treating every monster as equally weak. Test **Varn +1 Armor per body, Fyra +2 Armor and Kopita +2 Armor** first. Tumler still needs a separate combat-tuning pass. Keep Lemek, Muno, Dotra, Kurchin and Sooge at their current stats for now; inspect Sinodek's friendly banishments before adding durability.

The published gameplay change is **Tumler's pursuit fix only**. All Armor changes in this report are isolated experiments. No monster stats were changed.

**What was tested**

The baseline is `0d0983c78e4543a41e93aac11cd2fa5690ec70d7`, with monster rules `U13_MONSTERS_V8_HUNT_WAYPOINT`. The pursuit fix uses `U13_MONSTERS_V9_CLUSTER_PURSUIT`. The audit calls the accepted Python Marching engine and checks representative complete results against native Godot; it does not substitute a simplified damage model.

Each duel pairs one basic unit or one monster summon against each of the four basic types. Each squad trial adds the focal unit to a Butcher/Penitent/Vulture/Wright core, facing one of three fixed enemy squads: balanced, ranged-heavy, or a tightly grouped front line. The main sweep uses 24 seeds per matchup and both seats. Thus each unit's baseline squad averages below cover **144 trials**. Varn contributes its actual seeded 3–5 bodies per summon. Mixed fights put two monsters alongside each side's four-basic core.

The same identities, seed and placement are reused for paired variants. Units start movement-ready and fight for up to ten rounds, with normal regeneration, abilities, construction and goal retirement. There are no reinforcements or Lord/Castle/economy layers. Summoned monsters are **additional to the regular marchers generated by the committed cards** in the live game; these comparisons are not card-cost-normalized army values.

Enemy squad types have equal weight. Goal differences measure lane outcomes, not full-game victories. Surviving front-line units and stationary turrets are not expected to score at the same rate as back-line marchers. These are controlled screening results, not a population estimate of human match balance.

**Reading the roles**

- **Vulture** is the strongest basic damage/goal contributor in these squads: 6.83 HP damage and 53 goal arrivals. This is a candidate for monitoring, not sufficient evidence for a nerf: the mixed core supplies his protection.
- **Lemek, Muno and Dotra** each deal roughly 5.6–5.9 HP damage, before counting Armor damage. Lemek's 4 Armor and inexpensive recipe already distinguish him from a basic Butcher.
- **Kurchin** sustains 11.14 damage through HP and Armor versus Penitent's 8.47, and provides taunt. Low damage alone does not make him weak.
- **Sooge** deals 7.99 enemy HP damage, the highest baseline monster figure in these squads, plus 3.30 Armor damage. He also causes 0.69 friendly HP damage per summon. A rooted turret does not walk to the goal; zero goals are expected. A blanket durability buff is not the first issue to address.
- **Fyra** deals only 2.38 HP damage and averages 0.09 kills, but also 0.285 charms. He looks fragile in a squad despite his utility.
- **Kopita** heals only 0.132 actual HP per summon here. His start-of-round pulses often miss the period when allies need healing. Even +2 Armor only raises healing to 0.278; review pulse timing as well as durability.
- **Sinodek** averages 0.306 enemy banishments and 0.222 friendly banishments. Those are separate from his 0.60 combat kills. More Armor does not correct the friendly-removal cost.
- **Wright** contributes construction; his personal damage/goal totals are not a complete measure of his value.

**The flat +3 Armor proposal**

Armor is a consumable damage buffer. The experiment adds 3 starting and maximum Armor to every monster body. Sooge's turret conversion also becomes 9 Armor instead of 6, so rooting does not silently erase the proposed buff. Varn gains **9–15 Armor per summon** across its swarm.

All goal totals above use 144 summons. Tumler in this table retains the old pursuit to isolate Armor from pathing.

Varn's kills rise from **0.58 to 1.83 per summon** and his squad's mean goal advantage improves by **3.62**. The paired 95% bootstrap interval for that goal improvement is **3.18–4.02**. Lemek's squad gains **1.51** goals of advantage (1.32–1.73), while old-pursuit Tumler gains only **0.07** (−0.01–0.15). This is a large and uneven change, not a uniform correction to monster weakness.

Intervals use 2,000 paired bootstrap resamples, retaining both seats of each seed together and resampling within enemy composition. They describe seed variation in these fixed scenarios, not uncertainty over every possible army composition.

**Tumler: the reported behavior is real**

The old steering treated every nearby non-target enemy as an obstacle with a 180-unit detour radius. Overlapping detours around clustered enemies kept him circling and exposed to ranged fire. A durable-cluster regression reproduced this on both sides: he failed to enter the fight within the phase while the cluster remained alive.

In 128 focused clustered-target trials per variant:

| Pursuit and Armor | Trials with any landed hit | Mean first-hit tick, among trials that hit | HP damage per trial |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Old pursuit | 7 / 128 (5.5%) | 187.3 | 0.08 |
| Old pursuit, +3 Armor | 15 / 128 (11.7%) | 190.5 | 0.16 |
| Fixed direct pursuit | 128 / 128 (100%) | 34.7 | 0.90 |
| Fixed pursuit, +3 Armor | 128 / 128 (100%) | 34.7 | 2.42 |

These fixtures start closer to contact than the squad trials. Landing a hit is not the same as killing or reaching the originally preferred prey: a landed enemy melee attack can still intercept him, exactly as requested. In unopposed prey tests he kills the Vulture and reaches the goal in every trial. In screened/clustered fights his low durability and damage still matter.

The fix removes enemy-body detours, keeps slowing-pool avoidance, preserves 50% evasion while closing on a target, removes it at target contact, and retains melee-only interception. A second experimental route kept distant body detours, forced progress and committed within 360 units. It did not improve squad contributions (0.88 HP damage versus 0.92 for direct pursuit; zero kills for both), so it was discarded. That prototype was evaluated in Python only and has no native override in the audit tooling.

**Seat check and verification**

Across 120 equal-roster control setups, the player side led goals 10 times, the enemy led 13 times, and 97 tied; goal totals were 14 versus 18. This did not reproduce an always-winning player side. Most equal-unit duels mutually eliminate, so the many ties make this a limited fairness check, not a full-match win-rate estimate.

- Native regression: the new clustered-hunt assertions fail four checks before the change and pass after it. All **246 support/hunt checks** pass.
- **34 full native phases / 6,800 ticks** match Python exactly, including every visual tick and combat event, after the fix.
- Separate audit fixtures match complete native worlds and nonvisual events across **393 phases / 78,600 ticks**: 130 baseline, 134 +3 Armor, 129 fixed pursuit. These include all 14 unit types, construction, poison, charm, pulses, beams, rooting and banishment. Visual tick events are omitted from these 393 comparisons.
- **19 Python Marching tests** pass. A further **198 representative historical/control replays** reproduce every archived unit metric and goal count with the final audit tooling.
- Runtime used here: Godot **4.5.1 stable, Linux**, diagnostic binary `f62fdbde1`. The repository's **4.7.2 Windows** acceptance runtime was not executed here.

**Reproducing and inspecting the results**

The archive stores every unit's metrics for each trial, including HP and Armor damage, blocked/evaded damage, kills split into basic/monster victims, goals, deaths, survivors, regeneration, healing, charm, poison, banishments, construction, tower damage and first-hit timing. The JSON stores source fingerprints, parameters and per-body as well as per-summon aggregates. Archive SHA-256 is recorded in that JSON. The CSV contains per-summon summary rows; `mean_first_hit_tick` averages only bodies that actually landed a hit.

Original `current` rows in the archived main/hunt batches mean V8 before the fix. After updating, use the retained `legacy_hunt` controls to reproduce the old pursuit; `current` now means the shipped V9 behavior. Numeric outcomes are reproducible despite the updated rules fingerprint. No live rules are changed by choosing an audit variant.

```bash
# Old pursuit versus the original flat-Armor proposal.
PYTHONPATH=Scripts/Sim python -m u13_pysim.unit_balance \
  --output audit-main --samples 24 --workers 6 \
  --variants legacy_hunt legacy_hunt_armor3

# Pursuit comparison: use 64 seeds for each close-contact scenario.
PYTHONPATH=Scripts/Sim python -m u13_pysim.unit_balance \
  --output audit-hunt --samples 64 --workers 6 --modes hunt \
  --variants legacy_hunt legacy_hunt_armor3 current armor3

# Smaller buffs for the same focal squad slots.
PYTHONPATH=Scripts/Sim python -m u13_pysim.unit_balance \
  --output audit-small --samples 24 --workers 6 --modes squad \
  --focus Varn Fyra Kopita Tumler Dotra --variants armor1 armor2

# Check the shipped gameplay behavior in Godot.
godot --headless --path . --script res://Scripts/Sim/U13SupportHuntTestRunner.gd
```
