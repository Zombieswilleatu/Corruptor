> Windows accepted at clean `5e93ce0`: [uploaded evidence](evidence/U13_RITE_PLANS_WINDOWS_2026-09-18.json) passed 74 tests each under CPython 3.14.7 and PyPy 7.3.23, with identical reports/inputs for five games, 94 rounds and 2,361 operations. Invocation now reaches the complete-plan evaluator even when its immediate
card-cost score is zero or negative. The previous planner retained the proposal
but never assembled it. This fixes that admission bug; it does not claim to solve
every missed settlement opportunity.

The Python candidate is `U13_COMMON_SMART_CORE_ALPHA_V4_RITE_PLANS`. Game rules,
the accepted free opening, Godot BasicDoctrine, U12 and assets are unchanged.
This remains an experimental policy awaiting later shipping integration.

## Implementation

Each retained Rite gets two bounded alternatives: the Rite with conservation,
and the Rite with compatible ordinary choices. These are assembled before the
ordinary bundles consume the plan budget. The conservation alternative matters
because spending resources or consuming units elsewhere can change settlement.
Profane Ruins reserves its two Souls in the shared resource ledger.

Weights, the existing 70-point public settlement-scenario bonus and all work
limits are unchanged: 16 generated / four retained proposals per category,
32 complete plans, eight authority previews. Rites use the same legality
preview and hidden-order boundary as other plans. A public-board win scenario
is still uncertain and creates no hard veto against other plans.

New per-Rite diagnostics distinguish generated, retained, scored and selected
choices. Scored-plan win/loss counts describe the existing public scenario,
including repeated assembled alternatives before deduplication. A declined
retained Rite now reports complete-plan scoring or plan-budget exhaustion.
Historical policies' unmeasured scored-plan counts remain null.

## Matched comparison

The campaign ran 162 complete games: every one of the 81 ordered Lord matchups,
including mirrors, with V4 assigned to each seat once against frozen V3. Both
policies used the same accepted free-opening authority, default weights and
Keep / Stockpile / Summoning Circle / Siege Engine / Bastion loadout. The first
three castles start commissioned. The namespace was
`u13-rite-v4-2026-09-18`, repeat 00.

Each two-game pair keeps seed, Lords, loadout and seat assignments fixed while
swapping policies. Reversed Lord matchups share their unordered-pair seed, giving
45 seed clusters; the 162 games are not independent samples. This is one fixed
loadout and one seed repetition, not a balance or policy-strength campaign.

All 162 games completed: 2,916 rounds, mean 18 rounds, zero invalid operations,
rejected previews or censored games. Three paired setups were won by V4 in both
policy assignments; the other 78 split, and none were swept by V3. The overall
84–78 result is a small favorable observation, not an established strength gain.

All 19 selected Invocations produced their Tear. Seventeen occurred in a round
V4 won; two games continued. This association does not assign 17 additional wins
to Invocation. The campaign took 217.17 seconds with eight local worker processes,
including policy, simulation, recording and aggregation; this is not a PyPy
throughput benchmark.

## Remaining scoring issue

Twenty-one candidate decisions had a scored Invocation alternative whose public
scenario wins, but selected a plan whose scenario does not. The existing material
score can still outweigh the finite settlement bonus. These are possible misses,
not 21 proven lost wins: future opposing orders and reactions can change victory.
