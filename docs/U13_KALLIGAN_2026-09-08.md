# Kalligan: rules and discrete hazards

This slice adds Kalligan to the authoritative simulation and random-legal
frequency batches. Godot 4.7.2 stable is the runtime. The board picker and
Kalligan controls follow only after the local rules gate passes.

## Implemented behavior

- Inferno declares a public, next-round target: an enemy Lord Guard zone,
  the enemy shared Castle Guard zone, or either entire Marching lane.
- At Step 2 it creates Scorch, or relocates the existing instance. Scorch's
  stages are 1, 2, 1, then expiration. Moving preserves the original instance
  ID, declaration, activation round, age and expiration-bound cooldown.
- A relocation cannot be scheduled into the expiration round. It cannot
  accidentally create a fresh Scorch or reset its cooldown.
- Guard pulses defeat Guards whose value is at most the current intensity.
  Normal Guard pulses happen at Step 2, after advancement and relocation.
- Normal lane pulses happen at Step 11. All Marchers in that lane, including
  both owners and waiting Marchers, receive the discrete pulse. Armor absorbs
  first. Damage is environmental, with no combat-kill credit.
- Pyroclasm adds one pulse at Step 10F using the current Scorch location and
  intensity. It does not change its lifetime; the normal pulse still occurs.
- Forge-Repair restores 2 Integrity per eligible owned Castle at Step 3.
  Protected construction, Ruined and Profaned Castles are excluded. Restoration
  caps at the Castle's current maximum.
- Rapid Construction applies the same 2-Integrity restoration to both players
  while Kalligan is in the Breach. It does not add a second Forge-Repair pass.
- Rekindle observes exposed Defunct episodes and grants one Neutral Tear when
  an owned Castle from such an episode becomes operational. Maximum one per
  owner per round; simultaneous extra recoveries are consumed, not deferred.
  Initial construction/Commission is not a Defunct recovery.
- Armed powers survive source Banishment. New declarations and personal
  passives require the source Lord to be alive.

## Explicit interpretations of the handoff

The handoff specifies the Guard defeat thresholds but no separate normal
Guard pulse hook. This implementation uses Step 2 after lifecycle advancement
and prepared relocation.

Pyroclasm has no stated resource cost or cooldown in the handoff. It is free
and may be declared once each round while Scorch exists (activation cooldown
0 under the central once-per-round declaration rule).

For automatic restoration, exposed Defunct Castles are repairable standing
structures, distinct from Ruined/Profaned and protected construction. This
lets Forge-Repair carry a Castle from 0 through 2/4/6 to operational at 8 and
trigger Rekindle. These interpretations are recorded for rules review;
frequency results do not establish balance.

## Shared foundation changes

`U13Hazards` resolves deterministic discrete pulses through the existing
battle fact/reaction path. Stable pulse and hit IDs identify applications;
the match hook ledgers and persistent/pending registries own repetition.
Private reaction event views survive a public power resolution, including
hidden card draws triggered by a Guard defeat.

`U13Legality` accepts an opt-in authoritative persistent-effects context and
relocation rule. The match owner schedules relocation against the exact
existing effect ID without replacing its expiration clock. Snapshots validate
trusted stages, targets, original terms, relocation provenance, clocks and
phase ledgers. Failed previews/restores remain atomic.

Kalligan composes the existing Gremory, Deimos and Humbaba content. Older
content owners reject the new profile rather than silently missing its hooks.
No world copy of Scorch's lifetime is introduced.
