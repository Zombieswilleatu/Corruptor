## Rules

Choose one persistent Work Target, change it before submission, or clear it.
Each newly placed Guard in either zone supplies 1 work on that deployment.
A fresh Wright pair supplies an additional 3 once (two Wrights = 5 work).
Existing Guards do not generate deployment work again every round.
Work is settled after Guard placement in Development, before combat.

An unconstructed target gains 3 passive construction each round plus deployment
work. A damaged active Castle receives only work, without passive repair.
Full construction activates automatically. Completion clears the target; excess
work is lost, not banked or applied to another Castle. No selected target means
no work credit. Changing targets stops the previous project's passive progress.

Ruins cannot be repaired. A living Deimos can reconstruct his ruined Siege
Engine as protected construction, including passive 3. No other Lord or Castle
receives that exception. Existing repair locks still apply. Kalligan retains his
existing automatic Forge-Repair across damaged standing Castles.

Paid Construct/Repair/Activate choices are rejected in this profile. Repair
tokens remain fixed at zero only as an inert legacy schema field; no token is
created or spendable. The old Wright repair-payment exemption has no role.
The legacy modules remain available to earlier isolated U13 fixtures and U12 is
unchanged.

## Defensive bonds

Two newly placed matching Guards in the same zone establish a bond between
those exact physical cards and slots. If either leaves, dies, changes owner or
moves slots, the bond breaks permanently. A survivor plus a replacement does not
restore it. Two fresh Guards can establish a new bond. With three matching fresh
Guards, the two lowest slots form the pair and the third is unpaired.

| Pair | Benefit |
| --- | --- |
| Butcher | When its zone is attacked, destroy up to two distinct random enemy Marchers in the same lane before resolving the Guard layer. Empty lane: no kill. |
| Penitent | 3 protection before the Guard layer on each attack while intact. |
| Wright | +3 work once when the pair is deployed. |
| Vulture | Draw one additional card each subsequent round while intact, subject to the existing hand cap. |

Deployed Guard suits/values and intact pairs are public to both players and bots
under the subsequent accepted public-Guard policy. Unrevealed submissions remain
private; pair formation becomes public after Development. Triggered effects are
public. Butcher strikes resolve one at a time with normal death reactions, then choose a second surviving enemy if available. Selection uses deterministic keyed randomness so restore/replay agrees.

Combat commitments no longer gain the U13 same-suit +1 strength bonus. Ordinary
suit efficiencies remain. Ward recruits floor(printed suit total / 2) Marchers;
Hunt and Siege retain floor(printed suit total / 3). Fractions do not combine
across suits. Earlier U12 reward implementations are not reintroduced.

## Player interaction

The follow-up `U13_ACTION_FLOW_2026-09-14.md` places Work Target in the shared
action modal and sequences all round choices.

- Click **WORK TARGET**: eligible friendly Castles pulse. Click one to select it.
  Clicking the current target clears it; clicking the button again cancels the
  picker. There is no target-list menu or card payment panel.
- The planning readout shows the target, current/max Integrity, deployment/pair
  work, passive contribution and projected Integrity (including repair locks).
- Drag a hand card into either Guard zone to place it in a free Guard slot.
  Dropping onto a specific free slot uses that slot.
- Drag onto your Lord or a Castle to stage Ward in that lane.
- Bond badges and tooltips explain the benefit and the broken-pair rule.
  Aftermath reports work results, pair draws, protection and kills.
