# U13 Stockpile — Selective Stores

The game foundation runner now expects **7/7**. Stockpile joins Keep, Bastion,
Summoning Circle and Siege Engine in the authoritative game-conductor path.
This checkpoint adds the draw choice API and Random-Legal support; the playable
board still uses its exercise profile. Human modal wiring follows the full loop.

## Rules recovered

Sources: UI2 `CastlePowerText` and `RoundEngine._run_draw_step` with
`stockpile_filter` enabled. The historical flat +1 draw is not used.

- After a player's five ordinary draws, an operational Stockpile draws two extra.
- If both arrive, the player chooses one of those exact new cards to keep; the
  other is discarded. Existing hand cards cannot substitute for either card.
- The ten-card hand cap applies to each draw. With one space, draw and keep one;
  with no space, draw neither. Exhaustion can likewise leave zero or one extra.
- A damaged Stockpile below the operational floor, a protected project, a Ruined
  Castle or a Profaned Castle gives no benefit.
- Multiple operational Stockpiles grant one benefit, matching the old boolean
  Castle-power check. The lowest eligible slot identifies the source.
- Seat 0 completes its ordinary draws and selection before seat 1 draws. The
  discarded card is immediately available if the next draw recycles the discard.
- Existing U13 keyed recycling and ordinary-draw semantics remain authoritative.
  These Development draws do not invoke obsolete U12 Kanifous breach callbacks.

## Choice and save contract

`U13GameConductor.to_planning()` returns `game_draw_choice` with the chooser's
player ID when a choice is needed. Only that player's view exposes the offered
card IDs under `world.game_economy.stockpile_pending.card_ids`.
`choose_stockpile(player_id, keep_id)` resolves it. Calling `to_planning()` again
continues until another choice or ordinary planning.

`to_planning(true)` resolves choices using keyed Random-Legal selection through
that same API. It uses no old heuristic scoring or hidden opponent information.

`U13Match.submit_choice` delegates only to its configured content callback and
applies the result through a cloned owner, world validation and private event log.
Wrong-player, stale, non-offered and out-of-window choices leave the owner intact.
The public-state hook cannot advance while a choice is pending. Players cannot
submit powers, pay costs or commit orders before selection completes.

The pending cards remain in their owner's hand while planning is blocked. They
retain their ordinary stable identities and card-zone membership. Saves record
both the private offer and the draw-seat cursor. Duplicate/non-hand offers and
pending choices at the wrong hook are rejected on restore. Opponent projections
and events hide the kept card; the discarded card is public as usual.

The economy policy is now `U13_GAME_ECONOMY_V2`. Earlier game snapshots require a
new match. U12 and the existing U13 exercise-board profile are unchanged.

## Verification

The Stockpile suite uses explicit pre-match fixtures for operational Stockpiles.
It covers single/double Stockpiles, both players' choices, wrong-player and old-card
rejection, blocked advancement, private projections and selection events, malformed
save rejection, JSON replay through the rest of the round, Random-Legal choices,
full/nearly-full hands, below-floor/protected Stockpiles, exhausted decks, and
seat-ordered recycling of the selected discard into the next player's hand.

Economy and conductor/random replay regressions cover the normal opening and draw
path. Local tests use Godot 4.5.1 compatibility; the user runner remains pinned to
Windows Godot 4.7.2.

Remaining full-match work includes market decisions, waiter-to-Tear spending,
Veil and legitimate victory. These gates precede the full playable UI.
