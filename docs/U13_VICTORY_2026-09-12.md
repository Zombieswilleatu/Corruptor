# U13 victory resolution

The full-game profile now resolves victory at the end of Aftermath, after both
Vacant Throne awards. All sealed actions, Marching and round-end cleanup finish
before a winner is selected. There is no mid-round termination.

Veil threshold penalties and automatic drift remain **disabled**. Tear accounting,
Lord Breach powers and the two Tear-based victory conditions remain active.

## Rules

Thresholds and precedence follow `RuleConfig.lab_v6_5()` and the existing
`BotRoundEngine._check_win()` / `ResolutionFinaleEngine._check_win()` rules.
U13 gives them a single deterministic final-hook checkpoint.

| Priority | Victory | Requirement | Tie handling |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Ritual | At least 12 Souls and a living Lord | If both qualify, seat 0 wins, preserving existing seat order |
| 2 | FinalCollapse | Total Veil at least 26; higher Souls wins | Equal Souls favor seat 0; a living Lord is not required |
| 3 | Dominion | Total Veil at least 12; at least 5 personal Tears and strictly more than the opponent | Equal personal Tears produce no Dominion winner; a living Lord is not required |

Total Veil is neutral Tears plus both players' personal Tears. The old Humbaba
Seal modifier is not restored. A round cap never awards victory.

## Ownership and persistence

- `U13Victory` owns thresholds, evaluation, round clock and outcome validation.
- `U13GameContent` settles it after Vacant Throne and exposes the result to both
  players. Exactly one public `MATCH_FINISHED` event records the outcome and scores.
- `U13GameConductor.is_finished()` and `outcome()` expose terminal status.
  `finish_round()` and `to_planning()` report `game_finished` once resolved.
- The match owner rejects another round and immediate choices after termination;
  ordinary submission and hook windows are already closed. Reading the result
  repeatedly does not alter state or append events.
- JSON snapshots preserve the terminal outcome. Restore checks the victory clock,
  winner and reason against final scores and rejects a removed or forged outcome.
- The new policy prefix is `U13_VICTORY_V1`. Older full-game snapshots reject with
  a policy mismatch; start a fresh match. Earlier isolated U13 policies and frozen
  U12 authority keep their behavior.

## Gate

`run_u13_game.sh` now contains **18 runners**, including `U13VictoryTestRunner`.
The directed victory runner checks threshold boundaries, absent Lords, ties,
precedence, Pillage earning the twelfth Soul, Profane reaching Dominion/Final
Collapse, Vacant Throne grace rewards, exact replay at the winning hook, terminal
JSON restore, public outcomes, malformed saves and blocked continuation.

All 17 simulation runners passed locally on Godot 4.5.1, as did the isolated
match-owner regression suite. The eighteenth game runner is the pinned board
gate, which must run on Windows.

A separate standard-opening smoke run used seed `game-random-v1`, Kanifous
versus Orias, default Castle loadouts and Random-Legal planning for both seats.
It reached a real Dominion win for seat 1 (Orias) in round 18: personal Tears
4–5, total Veil 12, Souls 0–0. Terminal JSON restore reproduced the complete
snapshot and outcome exactly. No fixture resources or forced scoring actions
were injected; the diagnostic budget was 60 rounds with a 180-second watchdog.
This is one completed match, not a batch acceptance or balance claim.

Local Godot 4.5.1 compatibility checks do not replace the Windows Godot 4.7.2
acceptance gate. The broader 100–1,000 autonomous-match gate and candidate coverage
audit remain separate work; directed wins do not establish that gate or balance.
