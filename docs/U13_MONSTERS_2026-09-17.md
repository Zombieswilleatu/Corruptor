# Monster commitments and bounded Longevity — September 17, 2026

Implemented in the standalone U13 playable game and mirrored in the Python simulator. The accepted recipe baseline remains `FutureFeatures/Corruptor-Monster-Recipes-v0.1.md`; campaign unlock progression is not yet implemented. All ten recipes are unlocked in this prototype.

## Playing

Open **RECIPES** beside the top-left game controls for ingredients, stats, abilities and eligibility. In the **Combat** modal, commit cards to Hunt, Siege or Ward, then use **Summon …** to choose a qualifying monster. **No monster summon** remains an option. Extra cards are allowed; card values do not replace missing subjects.

Exactly one chosen recipe summons alongside the commitment's normal marchers, in the same lane. Cards spent on guards, work, Lord powers or rites, and cards saved in hand, do not count. Sooge and Sinodek have separate one-living-copy limits, without an additional cooldown. Resurrection and ownership transfers respect those limits; charm temporarily reserves the original owner's slot as well. The native and experimental Python bots choose a qualifying recipe from their committed cards; this is basic participation, not a recipe-saving strategy.

Monsters retain stable entity IDs, their own profiles, the existing approved sprite mappings, and the game's health/armor rings. Newly recruited bodies use the normal next-round movement hold and can defend when attacked. Save/replay preserves transformation, poison, hiding, targets and battlefield fields. Sinodek banishment does not produce death effects or resurrectable losses.

Distances use the existing 2400 × 600 lane coordinates. Speed translates to twice the displayed stat in fixed-point movement per tick. Monsters regenerate 1 HP per round under the existing regeneration rules; Varn's smaller HP keeps its 3–5 bodies from also becoming a large cheap health reserve. Ordinary subject-specific lamp boons are limited to ordinary subjects.

Sooge rolls once at the start of each Marching phase in which it is movement-ready. Its birth hold does not roll or build up chance; later summons still start at 25%. Each failed roll adds 15 percentage points for the next eligible round, capped at 100%. The per-unit attempt count and last roll round persist through save/load and resurrection; turret form remains permanent. This tuning uses monster rules version `U13_MONSTERS_V2` in both engines.

## Longevity and Inevitable Ruin

Longevity now restores an eligible active owned castle to **min(14, maximum Integrity)**. A castle already at or above that cannot be targeted. Firing rechecks eligibility; a no-op neither lowers health nor creates a Price. Protected construction and ruined/profaned castles stay ineligible. Normal and Breach Longevity share the cap and keep their respective Price weights.

Inevitable Ruin retains the prior patch: targets enemy active castles above 14 and reduces them to 14 after its existing delay. It cannot finish a castle or fire against one already at/below 14. The 14 target is conservative for Ruin's two-card cost and delay, while Longevity can restore considerably more health. Keep both at 14 for the first playtest; correctness tests do not establish that the two powers are equally valuable.

## Validation and continuation

Directed native tests cover recipes, missing ingredients, duplicate card IDs, overlapping choices, turret persistence, friendly fire, banishment/death separation, all ten monster profiles, Muno's once-per-round strike, ambushes, charm restoration, poison expiry, resurrection limits, the recipe page, and Longevity boundaries. Exact native/Python phase and admission comparisons accompany these checks. Full-game outcomes and verification counts are recorded separately under `docs/evidence/U13_MONSTERS_2026-09-17.json`.

Local validation uses the available **Godot 4.5.1 Linux diagnostic runtime**. Production still requires **Godot 4.7.2 stable**; its runtime guard is unchanged. In-engine visual acceptance on the user's Windows machine remains. Start a **new game** after updating: the rules/policy fingerprint changed, so earlier-rule saves are intentionally incompatible.
