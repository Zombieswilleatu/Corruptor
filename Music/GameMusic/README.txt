Put your WAV, MP3 or Ogg Vorbis music files directly in this folder.
Export MIDI from the Music Lab as WAV first. No Godot reimport is needed.
New tracks are discovered at the next playlist cycle or new match.
The playlist waits silently if this folder is empty.
Tracks repeat in full until their total playing time reaches at least four minutes.
Each song plays before the shuffled list starts again.
settings.cfg: volume_percent is 0-100 (default 50); enabled=false turns game music off.
Settings take effect the next time the playable board is opened.
Original recordings are never changed.
